credentials are needed for the simulator's offline synthetic demo.

SAFETY/PRODUCT LIMITS
Prototype heuristics, not validated fatigue diagnosis. Foreground monitoring
only. Camera loss/dim light/background show tracking unavailable. No fictional
dhaba distances or coffee-as-cure claims. Alerts direct the driver to stop safely
and rest. A reply does not mean the driver is safe to continue.
